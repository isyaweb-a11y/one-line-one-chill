One line of horror: a single sentence of under 200 characters that has to frighten a reader in one breath:

> My smart speaker answered a question I only thought, in my mother's voice, and asked why I stopped talking to her.

`main.py` composes the line from a setup and a payoff, gates it to 1–200 characters on a single line, and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
